Nº 43 · The window

Nose againstthe glass.

Facturas, alfajores, empanadas and cakes, all ready to take away.

How it started

It began witha car full ofalfajores.

During the pandemic, Florencia baked Argentinian treats for family and friends in a café kitchen lent to them after hours, and drove them around Dublin herself. In August 2021, she and Benjamin, both from Buenos Aires, opened Nº 43.

To stay

Bakeology In · Nº 58/59 Meath Street

Dine-in only

Across the road

A differentstory.

Bakeology In opened in July 2025 on the corner across the street: a café to sit down in, with brunch, empanadas, coffee and cakes, and a menu of its own.

Bakeology In · Nº 58/59

Pull up a chair.

Dine-in only. Food from Nº 43 is takeaway, so it can't be eaten in here: two places, two menus.

  • Brunch until 3 pm
  • Coffee and cakes all day
  • Empanadas until half an hour before closing
  • Lemonade served in a ceramic penguin
  • Wednesday to Friday, 10 to 4
  • Saturday and Sunday, 10 to 5
  • Closed Monday and Tuesday
